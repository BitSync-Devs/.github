# BitSync Devs 🚀

**BitSync Devs** is a collaborative tech organization focused on **building real-world solutions while learning and growing together**. We bridge **hardware and software development**, delivering practical services while continuously improving our skills as developers and engineers.

---

## 🌐 What We Do

At BitSync Devs, we believe in *learning by building*. Our projects range from embedded systems and IoT prototypes to desktop applications that integrate seamlessly with hardware.

---

## 🔧 Hardware Services

We design and develop hardware-based solutions, including:

* **Arduino Uno projects**
* **ESP32-based systems**
* **IoT-enabled hardware prototypes**
* **Custom PCB design and layout**
* **Sensor-based monitoring and control systems**
* **Hardware–software integrated solutions**

These projects are suitable for academic, prototype, and small-scale production use.
